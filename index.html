What it does

A short list of what is in the outline. Nothing more.

  • Two-sided conversation

    Each person gets a pane aimed at them. Speak on your side; read the other person in your language.

  • A language per person

    Pick the language each person speaks and reads. The other side shows the translation.

  • On-device speech and translation

    Speech recognition and translation are intended to run on the device with Apple’s Speech and Translation frameworks.

  • Face-to-face or side-by-side

    On a regular iPhone, use a face-to-face split (top half rotated 180°) or a side-by-side / Duo layout.

  • No account

    No sign-in. Conversation text is meant to stay on the device.
