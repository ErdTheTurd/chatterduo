Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

This policy describes the Chatterduo iPhone app and this website. Chatterduo is made by Edward Dunn.

No account

Chatterduo does not require an account. There is no cloud account to create, and you do not sign in to use the app.

Microphone

The app uses the microphone so each person can speak. That audio is used for speech recognition on the device. The app is not designed to upload your conversation audio to Edward Dunn.

Speech recognition and translation

Speech recognition and translation are intended to run on-device using Apple’s Speech and Translation frameworks. Conversation text is meant to stay on the device.

If a language pack must be downloaded, that download is handled by the system. Those packs are not sent to us.

What stays on your device

Conversation text shown in the app is meant to remain on your device. This outline does not include a Chatterduo server that stores your chats.

No analytics or advertising SDKs

This outline does not include analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not sell your data.

This website

This site is a static marketing site. It does not collect form data. There is no contact form that posts information to a server.

Like most websites, your browser may send standard technical requests (such as your IP address) to the host that serves these pages. That is ordinary web hosting behavior; this site itself does not add tracking pixels or analytics scripts.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@chatterduo.com.

If this policy changes, the updated text will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
