FAQ

What is Chatterduo?

Chatterduo is an iPhone app for two people talking across one phone. Each person reads the conversation in their own language on their side of the screen.

How does it work on iPhone Duo vs a regular iPhone?

On iPhone Duo, the two panes can sit in a Duo / side-by-side layout. On a regular iPhone, you can use a face-to-face split (top half rotated 180° so someone across the table can read) or switch to a side-by-side layout.

Where does speech recognition and translation run?

They are intended to run on-device with Apple’s Speech and Translation frameworks. Conversation text is meant to stay on the device.

Do I need to download language packs?

Some languages may need a system language pack. That download is handled by the system and is not sent to us.

Why does the app ask for microphone access?

So each person can speak. The microphone is used for speech recognition. See the Privacy Policy for more detail.

Is Chatterduo on the App Store yet?

Not yet. It is coming soon to the App Store. This site is an outline while the app is still being finished.
